# Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard

This repository contains a Power BI project focused on analyzing sales performance using a comprehensive dataset. The dashboard provides insights into annual sales, product performance, and customer behavior, enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## Dataset

The dataset used for this project was sourced from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets). It includes the following fields:

* `InvoiceNo`
* `StockCode`
* `Description`
* `Quantity`
* `InvoiceDate`
* `UnitPrice`
* `CustomerID`
* `Country`
* `Discount`
* `PaymentMethod`
* `ShippingCost`
* `Category`
* `SalesChannel`
* `ReturnStatus`
* `ShipmentProvider`
* `WarehouseLocation`
* `OrderPriority`

---

## Power BI Report Pages & Visuals

The Power BI report is organized into three interactive pages, each designed to provide specific analytical insights. Each page allows filtering for a single calendar year and features a sliding hamburger menu for improved navigation.

### 1. Sales Dashboard: Annual Sales Summary

This page offers a high-level overview of annual sales performance.

* **Key Metrics:**
    * Quantity Sold (Current Year vs. Last Year)
    * Net Sales (Current Year vs. Last Year)
    * Total Customers (Current Year vs. Last Year)
* Net Sales by Channel
* Net Sales by Category
* Transaction Count by Country
* Net Sales Over Time

### 2. Product Sales Analysis

This page delves into the performance of individual products and categories.

* Net Sales vs. Units Sold by Category
* Top 5 and Bottom 5 Products Sold
* Units Sold Over Time by Category

### 3. Customer Analysis

This page focuses on understanding customer behavior and their impact on sales.

* Net Sales vs. Units Sold by Country
* Net Sales by Country and Category
* Unique Customers by Month
